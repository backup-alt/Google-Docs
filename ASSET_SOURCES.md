The interface uses locally bundled assets so icons and text do not depend on a network connection at runtime.

- Material Symbols Outlined, optical size 20, weight 400: Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`).
- Roboto: Google Fonts.
- Google Docs and Gemini product marks: `https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/1x/`.
- Calendar, Keep, Tasks, Contacts, and Maps companion icons: `https://www.gstatic.com/companion/icon_assets/`.

The download scripts in `scripts/` record the exact asset URLs. Toolbar symbols are rendered through the official icon font; product icons are the supplied Google image assets.
